What Is the Ender Gauntlet?

The Ender Gauntlet is a mythical item forged from obsidian and infused with the essence of the Ender Dragon. It grants control over space, time, and teleportation.

Abilities & Powers

  • Void Step: Teleport 50 blocks instantly.
  • Phase Shift: Pass through walls for 5 seconds.
  • Dragon’s Grasp: Summon spectral claws with knockback.
  • Ender Recall: Return to spawn with no cooldown.

🕳️ Chrono Blink: Freeze time for 3 seconds.

How to Find It

  1. Defeat the Ender Dragon twice.
  2. Locate the Obsidian Crypt beneath the End Gateway.
  3. Solve the Chrono Puzzle with Ender Pearls.
  4. Survive the Void Warden miniboss.
